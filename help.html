Help / How to use Zavora SMS

👋 Welcome to Help

Everything you need to know about buying numbers, depositing funds, and using Zavora SMS.

1 How to Buy a Number

  1. Make sure your wallet has enough balance.
  2. Go to Dashboard.
  3. Select a service (e.g. WhatsApp) from the grid.
  4. Select a country (e.g. USA) from the grid.
  5. Click the blue "Buy SMS" button at the bottom.
  6. Your number will appear in Recent Orders — copy the number.
  7. Use the number to receive your verification code.
  8. Once the code arrives, copy the code.
  9. You will be charged only when the SMS arrives.
→ Go to Dashboard

2 How to Deposit Money

  1. Click Deposit in the menu or on the dashboard.
  2. Click "Generate Virtual Account" and wait 3–5 seconds.
  3. Copy the bank account details shown.
  4. Make a transfer from your bank app to that account.
  5. Return and click "I Have Paid".
  6. Fill in the amount, sender name, and transaction reference.
  7. Submit. Your deposit will be approved within 1–10 minutes.
  8. Your wallet will be credited after admin approval.
→ Go to Deposit

3 Where is My Number?

Once you buy a number, it appears in Recent Orders on the Dashboard.

To see all your previous numbers, click My Orders in the menu.

→ View My Orders

4 The Number Didn't Work

No problem! If the SMS hasn't arrived yet, you can cancel the order and you won't be charged.

If you've already received a bad code, contact support for a refund.
